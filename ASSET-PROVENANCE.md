# Assets and provenance

All 3D meshes, furniture assemblies, humanoid rig, procedural poses, wood/fabric/tile/wallpaper/rug textures and audio synthesis are original code authored for this prototype. Unity built-in primitives and Standard shader are used as construction inputs. No purchased asset packs or copied game content.

The family oil portrait at `Assets/Resources/FamilyPortrait.png` was generated with the built-in image_gen tool. Prompt: “Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: framed family portrait painting used as a 3D game texture. Create a single finely painted 1920s oil portrait of a family in an English country manor: stern older father seated in a dark burgundy velvet chair at left, elegant wife in muted ivory and teal at center, young adult daughter standing to right with a small red cameo pendant, and a serious young adult son behind. All adult faces visible. A black dog seated near the chair. Half to three-quarter length group composition, oil painting brushwork, slightly faded varnish, subtle craquelure, warm amber and desaturated teal, tasteful uneasy gothic mood but clearly readable. No frame, no border, no text, no watermark. Landscape aspect ratio 3:2. This is a real in-game decorative oil painting, not concept art of a room.”

UI fonts: Noto Sans Regular and Noto Serif Regular from https://github.com/notofonts/noto-fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1); see FONT-LICENSE.txt. Files are named Interface.ttf and Editorial.ttf for stable Resources references. No Windows system fonts are distributed.

Unity 6.3 editor/runtime is subject to Unity's license. The project uses only bundled Unity modules; it needs no external networking package, account service, or paid assets.
